Flying : practical training for intermediates : (Notes 1997-2002)
Luca Buvoli ; with an essay by Stuart Horodner.
Author: Buvoli, Luca.
Published: [Portland, Or.] : Portland Institute for Contemporary Art : New York, NY : Distributed by Distributed Art Publishers, c2002.
Description: 33, xiii p. : col. ill. ; 23 x 31 cm.
Format: Book
Summary:
Though mankind has made many advances in the past few centuries, we have not quite mastered the art of flying, certainly not to the extent that birds have. Thus Luca Buvoli's notes, research, and diagrams on the art of flying are greatly appreciated, especially this uniquely assembled artist's book, in which he offers tips for the intermediate flyer.
