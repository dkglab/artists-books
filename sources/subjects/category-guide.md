# Mostly deferred to wikidata for terms with multiple names and what category of geographic. Getty thesaurus of geographic names also used for determining geographic category especially for historic and inhabited place

Parenthesis for dates only. Quotes and comma for subdivisions "Aesthetics, Modern".

Geographic
    city (smaller units like streets might collapse to here or to inhabited places like specific burroughs that include the street)
    continent
    country (Australia goes here)
    historical (empires that are gone, ancient cites that no longer exist and are not inhabited or subject is the ancient version)
    inhabited place (areas like communes or neighborhoods that are geographic like Assisi, Italy but do not fit other categories)
    island
    landform
    park (natural area whose borders are only defined by governments)
    region (defined area that doesn't fit other categories like Oceania)
    state-province (does not need to be called a state or province but fits the broader category of state or province on wikidata)
    structure (manmade geographic structure like a bridge)
    water

Topical
    people (types of people, professions, actions specific to humans)
    objects
    concept (includes language concepts like alphabet)
    arts (terms specific to an art form like African American art or Aleatoric music, terms like abstraction that are used in multiple study areas are concepts)
    phenomena (includes sensorial phenomena that are not specific to humans or can be used as a concept or human medical term like afterimage)
    condition (includes diseases)
    environment (natural enivronment but not geographical like air, period table elements)
    event (not time bound like accident)

Chronological
    event (time bound)
