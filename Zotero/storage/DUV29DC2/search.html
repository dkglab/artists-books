Description
Language:English
Notes:"In the summer of 2008, I pulled four, half-buried objects from the clay bank of Alabama's Black Warrior River. Soon thereafter, I sent each of three poets one of these artifacts, along with the request that it serve as the origin of a manuscript. (The remaining object, I would use as the source for my own manuscript.) ... The project is an attempt, from the midst of a transient culture, to overcome the limitations distance places on creative exchange; and to produce something that is, nonetheless, rooted in a specific place."--Inside cover of box.
Condition: no library markings, in protective enclosure.
Four parts bound in printed and illustrated paper wrappers, printed with the word Any, Thing, From, Anywhere, on the cover of parts 1-4 respectively. The four booklets are housed in a cloth-covered clamshell box. Explanation of the project, notes on the authors, and production information printed on lining papers in box. Emily Tipps is the proprietor of High5 Press and completed this project for her MFA in the Book Arts from the University of Alabama.
Library copy is number 26 in an edition of 60 numbered copies.
