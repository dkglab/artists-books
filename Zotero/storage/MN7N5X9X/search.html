Art Library has copy no. 148.
Black card covers with a cut-paper overlay, hand-tinted in green, pink, lavender, yellow, buff and terracotta, depicting an imaginary landscape with whimsical and grotesque figures. The book is covered with a rectangle of blotched buff cotton fabric. The fabric cover is knotted at the corners with beige yarn and has cut-out letters spelling out the title on the upper portion and author's name on the lower portion. When the cords are tied together at the upper and lower corners, the book is enclosed in the fabric. The black paper end papers have hand-tinted cut-outs of female faces, the lower of which includes the statement of limitation. Reproduced typescript and decorative borders on white bond pages.
Condition of Art Library copy: no library markings, in protective enclosure.
Limited ed. of 200 copies.
One of a series of handmade books published by Ediciones Vigía.
