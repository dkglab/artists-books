Series: Colección del San Juan.

Description
Language:Spanish
Notes:"...consta de doscientos ejemplares manufacturados e iluminados a mano por las Ediciones Vigía en su Colección Del San Juan. Para la realización fueron usados papeles y cartulinas de diferentes texturas y gramajes, colores acrílicos y textiles. Las llaves que aparecen en cada ejemplar fueron traídas por amigos y miembros del taller editorial"--Colophon.
At head of title: "Premio América Bobia 2013."
Booklet issued in folder-style stiff paper cover covered with mottled green, orange, blue, and yellow cloth; collage on upper cover includes cutouts of three human figures with animal heads, ornamental panel with poet's name, and title letters mounted on a triangle of corrugated cardboard; wrapped around the three figures in center is a gold metal key on white string; lower cover decorated with cutouts of publisher's lamp logo, publisher's name, and series title; white lining papers printed with rows of animal silhouettes in black.
Poem printed on brown paper which has border of animal silhouettes printed in black. Preceding the poem, which begins on page 7, are three illustrated leaves: the first is printed on both sides with rows of animal silhouettes in black, with a large keyhole cut out in center; the second is the title page illustrated on recto with a black keyhole and key, and on the verso, with drawing of a group of animals in shape of a keyhole; the third leaf takes you through the keyhole to the world of "Zoológico" on a second title page bearing name of illustrator, and bordered along upper and lower edges with brown paper animal cutouts; colophon page at end decorated with cutout of publisher's lamp logo enhanced with orange, yellow, blue, and gold metallic acrylic paint.
