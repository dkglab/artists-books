Dia Al-Azzawi : a retrospective, from 1963 until tomorrow
text: Dia al-Azzawi [and 3 others] ; curator: Catherine David ; translation: Karim Traboulsi, Nariman Youssef.
Author: ʻAzzāwī, Ḍiyāʼ, artist.
Published: Cinisello Balsamo (MI), Italy: Silvana ; Doha (Q) : Mathaf: Arab Museum of modern art, 2017.
Edition: First edition in Italy.
Description: 493 pages : illustrations (chiefly color) ; 27 cm
Format: Book
Summary:

This catalog accompanies the first major retrospective of London-based Iraqi artist Dia Al-Azzawi (born 1939). Working in a variety of two- and three-dimensional mediums over his 55-year career, including his mural-like political paintings and assemblage works, Al-Azzawi has become one of Iraq's most influential artists.
