Description
Language:Spanish
Notes:"Un libro-arte de Estévez."
Consists of a single scroll formed from joined sheets of white bond paper. The text is the poem "Eternidad" hand-written in Spanish, English and French and framed with colored hand-painted borders. The scroll also contains full-color hand-painted illustrations, some using cutouts and collage elements. When rolled up the scroll is secured with a separate hand-painted wrapper.
Edition of 50 hand-made items.
Notes:Spanish, English and French.
